5. Data Retention

Settings and tweak history stay on your device until you delete them or uninstall the Software. Diagnostic reports and support messages are kept only as long as needed to resolve the issue, typically no longer than 12 months, and are then deleted. Hosting logs are kept according to our hosting provider’s own policies.
