Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what information the NextGen Discord bot (“NextGen,” “the Bot,” “we,” “us”) collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using NextGen, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

NextGen is designed to collect as little personal information as possible. Depending on how the Bot is used, we may process:

Data typeWhy it’s collected
Discord user IDTo identify who issued a command (e.g. skip, queue, volume)
Server (guild) ID and voice/text channel IDsTo know where to play audio and respond to commands
Command name and timestampBasic logging for debugging and abuse prevention
Song/queue requests (track titles, URLs)To build and play the playback queue

We do not read, store, or log the content of your Discord messages beyond the command text itself, and we do not record or store voice channel audio.

3. How We Use Information

  • To operate core features such as playing, queuing, and skipping tracks;
  • To maintain per-server settings (e.g. default volume, DJ role);
  • To diagnose errors and prevent abuse of the Bot;
  • To improve reliability and performance over time.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use your data for advertising.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

To function, NextGen communicates with:

  • Discord, via its official API, to receive commands and send responses;
  • Music sources such as YouTube and Spotify, to fetch audio and track metadata requested by users;
  • Our hosting provider, which stores server infrastructure and any logs described above.

We do not share your data with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

5. Data Retention

Playback queue data is held in memory only and is cleared when the queue ends or the Bot restarts. Per-server settings persist until a server administrator removes the Bot or requests deletion. Debug logs are retained for a limited period (typically no longer than 30 days) before being automatically deleted.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect the limited data NextGen processes. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children’s Privacy

NextGen is intended for use in accordance with Discord’s own age requirements. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age permitted by Discord’s Terms of Service.

8. Your Rights
